# Your Odhvica E-commerce Store

## A complete online store for your handmade brand

Odhvica gives your business an independent branded online store for selling handmade fashion, bags, accessories and related products. Your store is built for your own customers, your own domain and your own business operations.

It is not a marketplace. Your customers shop directly from your brand website.

## What you receive

| Included | What it means for your business |
|---|---|
| Live branded website | A customer-facing online store on your agreed domain, designed around your brand identity, products and content. |
| Storefront redesign | Your logo, colours, typography, product photography, page layout, collections and brand story are set up for your business. |
| Admin access | A secure admin area where authorised team members manage daily store work. |
| Product and collection management | Add/edit products, prices, variants, stock, images, descriptions, collections and product information. |
| Order management | View orders, payment status, customer details, production notes, shipping/tracking and support requests. |
| Customer tools | Manage customer accounts, addresses, order history, customer-support requests and approved marketing preferences. |
| Sales and promotion tools | Manage sale prices, discount codes, selected promotions and free-shipping rules where included in your agreed scope. |
| Payment and shipping setup | Your agreed payment methods and shipping settings are configured using accounts approved for your business. |
| Store policies and content | Manage brand story, FAQs, size/care guides, shipping/returns pages and other customer-facing content. |
| Launch and handover | Testing, agreed admin training, launch checklist and access handover for the deployed store. |

## What you own and what stays with the developer

| Your business owns | The developer retains/manages |
|---|---|
| Your brand name, logo, products, product photos and business content. | The reusable Odhvica master source code and shared platform framework. |
| Your domain, subject to your registrar account arrangement. | Reusable internal components, processes and documentation used to build and maintain stores. |
| Your customer relationship, catalogue, prices, policies and store-specific data. | The right to reuse the master platform for other independent client stores. |
| Your payment, courier, email, analytics and advertising accounts, unless another written arrangement is agreed. | Technical maintenance tools and internal development workflow used to support the deployed store. |
| The deployed website and agreed level of admin access for the duration of your service agreement. | Source-code delivery, unless a separate written source-code licence or handover agreement is made. |

> You receive a live deployed store and the agreed access to operate it. The default package does not transfer the reusable master source code.

## What you can manage through the admin area

Authorised store owners and staff can manage routine business tasks without waiting for a developer. Access levels can be limited for team members who only need to manage content, fulfilment or customer support.

| Area | Typical actions |
|---|---|
| Products | Create products, update descriptions, prices, variants, images, stock, size/care information and availability. |
| Orders | Review new orders, check payment status, view customer notes, manage production status, add tracking and handle support actions. |
| Customers | View relevant customer profiles, order history, addresses and approved marketing preferences. |
| Content | Update banners, collections, pages, FAQs, policy text, brand story and selected campaigns. |
| Promotions | Create or manage approved discount codes, sale dates and configured shipping offers. |
| Store settings | Manage approved shipping, payment, notification and staff settings. Some sensitive settings may require owner approval or technical support. |
| Reports | Review available sales, product, customer, promotion and operations reports. |

## Support and maintenance

Your agreed support plan will state what is included and what is charged separately. Normal support may include platform updates, security maintenance, backup/monitoring checks, bug fixes within the agreed scope and help with standard store operations.

A new feature, a different business workflow, a new outside service, a major storefront redesign or a custom report may require separate scope, pricing and delivery approval. This keeps the core store stable and avoids unplanned changes to your live business.

| Support area | Typical responsibility |
|---|---|
| Platform stability and technical maintenance | Developer, under the agreed support plan. |
| Daily products, prices, orders, customers and content | Your authorised store team. |
| Payment/courier/email/analytics account ownership and billing | Your business, unless another written agreement says otherwise. |
| Major new feature or custom workflow | Defined and approved separately before work starts. |
| Business policies, product claims and legal decisions | Your business with appropriate professional advice. |

## Your business responsibilities

The store provides tools and secure workflows, but your business remains responsible for the accuracy and legality of the information and decisions you publish or use.

Before launch and whenever business details change, you are responsible for providing and approving accurate product information, pricing, shipping/returns terms, customer-support details, business identity, tax information, consent wording and any country-specific selling rules. You are also responsible for maintaining eligible payment, courier, email, analytics and advertising accounts.

You should obtain appropriate legal and tax advice for your actual business, selling countries, customer data, tax invoices, customs, consumer rights, returns rules, product claims and privacy notices. The website can support these processes, but it does not replace professional legal, tax or regulatory advice.

## Working together for a successful launch

A successful launch needs both technical readiness and business readiness. The developer prepares the store, testing and deployment. Your business prepares the products, images, policies, account approvals, operational staff and customer-service process. The store is launched only after the agreed checklist and business sign-off are complete.
